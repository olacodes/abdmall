How trading on abdmall works

Buyers and sellers, with abdmall in the middle · October 2026

abdmall is a place where people buy and sell from each other — peer to peer, with abdmall as the moderator. abdmall holds the money, the goods pass through its warehouse, and the buyer sees the item before anyone is paid. Neither side has to trust the other; they only have to trust abdmall.

1 A sale, step by step

1
A seller lists an itemSellers are met and vetted by abdmall before they can list anything.
2
A buyer pays into abdmallThe money is held by abdmall. The seller has not been paid. The item is reserved so nobody else can buy it.
3
abdmall tells the seller to bring the goods inThis is the point of taking payment first: the seller knows the buyer is serious before making the trip.
4
The buyer comes to the warehouse and inspects the itemIn person, with their own eyes, before committing.
5
If the buyer is happy, they sign for it and take itabdmall then pays the seller, keeping its commission.
6
If the buyer is not happy, nobody is paidThe item goes back to the seller and the buyer is refunded.
Why this is better than the usual arrangement

The buyer never sends money to a stranger. The seller never hands over goods to one. And because the buyer inspects the item themselves, abdmall never has to judge whether a product is good enough — the person who is paying for it decides that.

2 The rules that make it work

Two deadlines and two ways a sale can fall through. Every case has an answer, so nothing sits unresolved:

SituationWhat happens
Seller has 48 hours to deliver to the warehouse If they miss it, the buyer is refunded automatically and the seller gets a strike.
Buyer has 5 days to come and inspect If they do not come, the item goes back to the seller and the buyer gets a strike.
Buyer rejects because the item is not as described Full refund. The seller gets a strike — they listed it wrongly.
Buyer rejects because they changed their mind Full refund, but the buyer gets a strike — the seller made that trip for nothing.

Repeat strikes mean losing access to abdmall. This matters most to sellers: they are the ones who spend time and transport on every sale, and they will stop using abdmall if buyers waste their trips without consequence.

Something to watch

We suggest not deducting any money from a buyer's refund at first, even when the wasted trip is their fault — taking money off a refund causes more arguments than it is worth. Strikes alone should be enough while volumes are small. If wasted trips become common, a small fee paid to the seller can be added later. It is much easier to introduce one than to remove one.

The seller only pays when they are paid, which makes abdmall easy to join. Nothing is charged for a sale that falls through.

4 How the money moves

Every sale gets its own bank account number. When a buyer decides to buy, our payment provider creates a one-off account number for that order alone, and the buyer transfers to it. Because the number belongs to that one sale, the payment arrives already matched — there is no reference to quote and nothing for the buyer to mistype.

The money lands in abdmall’s escrow account and sits there untouched. When the buyer accepts the goods, abdmall transfers 90% to the seller and moves its 10% across to the business account. If the sale falls through, the full amount goes back to the buyer by transfer.

Bank transfer rather than card. A card payment can be reversed by the buyer’s bank months after the event. In this model the buyer has already walked out with the goods and the seller has been paid, so a reversal would be a straight loss to abdmall. Transfers cannot be undone that way. Cards can still be offered for low-value items where the risk is small.

The escrow money needs its own bank account. It belongs to the buyer until they accept the goods, and to the seller afterwards. It must never share an account with abdmall’s own money — rent, salaries, the warehouse. Commission crosses over only when a sale completes.

Seller bank details are collected and checked at vetting, not when there is money to send. Discovering a wrong account number while trying to pay someone is the worst possible moment.

One number to watch, daily

The escrow account should always hold exactly the total of sales paid for but not yet completed. If the two ever differ, something has gone wrong and it should be looked at that day rather than at month end.

5 What this means for abdmall

  • A room and someone to run it. Goods only arrive after a buyer has paid, and leave within days, so this is a holding room rather than a warehouse full of stock.
  • Meeting every seller. This is the strongest protection in the whole design. Because you have met them, a strike means something and a seller taking money and disappearing is far less likely.
  • The money being held is not abdmall's. It belongs to the buyer until they accept the goods, and to the seller after that. It needs its own bank account that is never spent from.
  • One judgement to make. When a buyer rejects an item, someone decides whether it was genuinely not as described, or the buyer simply changed their mind. That is the only call abdmall has to make, and it is made with the item and the listing photographs both in front of you.
Worth settling early

One warehouse means one city. The website must make the collection location clear before anyone pays, or you will spend your time refunding buyers who did not realise they had to travel.

6 Legal and paperwork

WhatWhy
A conversation with your bank and a lawyer Holding money on behalf of other people is regulated in Nigeria. Many platforms operate this way — it is about doing it knowingly rather than discovering it at volume.
A written agreement sellers accept when they join The 10% commission, the 48-hour deadline, what a strike means, and that abdmall decides whether a rejection was fair.
Terms buyers accept when they buy The 5-day collection window, what happens if they do not come, and that inspecting the item is their responsibility.
A signed receipt at collection The buyer signs for the item as they take it. This is the record that protects abdmall if a sale is ever disputed afterwards.
A word with your accountant Nigeria has withholding tax obligations when a business pays vendors. The 10% commission is also business income and should be recorded separately from money held in escrow.
Accounts in the company’s registered name Both the escrow bank account and the payment provider account should be in abdmall’s name, matching the CAC documents exactly.
