3. Target audience

Mobile-first shoppers aged roughly 18–45 who buy online regularly but want more payment flexibility and clearer delivery tracking than they get today. Primary personas worth writing for: the deal-seeker (motivated by price and flash sales), the convenience buyer (wants speed and reliability), and the cautious first-timer (needs trust signals before their first purchase).
